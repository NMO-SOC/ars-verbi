Writing the Conclusion

Conclusion

If you think of a conclusion as less of summarising everything, and more of combining all of these conflicting ideas and messages into one overall conclusive point. We can do this by using the following structure.

Structure

The structure for a conclusion can follow the same structure as an introduction. However, instead of introducing the text, you are concluding your discussion. Here is an alternative method: Topic:

‘In Jackson’s stories, characters conform because they are afraid of being excluded.’ Do you agree?

Conclusion Structure for Analytical Essay Writing
Purpose in Conclusion Example Sentence
Overview of how the whole text demonstrates the key concepts in the essay topic Fundamentally, The Lottery and Other Stories explores the pressure to belong, and how readily ordinary people give up their own judgement to avoid standing apart.
Summary of the complications of this topic Throughout the collection, Jackson complicates the idea that fear alone drives conformity, showing that habit, tradition and the pleasure of belonging can be just as powerful, as the villagers of ‘The Lottery’ reveal.
Focus on the key authorial message in conclusive language Therefore, Jackson warns that a community which punishes difference turns its members into the enforcers of its prejudice, as Mrs Winning becomes in ‘Flower Garden’.

The Initial Concluding Sentence

Here is a list of concluding and verb phrases you can use at the beginning of your conclusion.

Concluding Sentence Starters and Verb Phrases
Concluding Phrase Verb Phrase
